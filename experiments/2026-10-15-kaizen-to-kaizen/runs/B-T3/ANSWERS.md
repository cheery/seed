# Answers from the person who asked for the change

Written before any arm ran (by the session that built this experiment,
at Henri's word "your call on the answers").  The person is not
available beyond this file.

- **Data.** `method/reviews.log` is the review history: every review so
  far, with request and answer times, rounds, comments, and how many
  comments proposed a change.  It is all there is.
- **T1, comments propose a concrete change** — meant to cut the number
  of review rounds per change.
- **T2, answered within 24 hours** — meant to cut the wait from request
  to first answer.
- **T3, a fixed reviewer for a new contributor's first three changes** —
  meant to cut the rounds a new contributor's changes take.
