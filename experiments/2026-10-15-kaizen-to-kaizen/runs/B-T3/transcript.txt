**Baseline.** I took this from `method/reviews.log`. The measure is the average number of review rounds per change, over each new contributor's first three changes. There are 7 such changes so far (kai, mo and lumi), averaging **2.57 rounds**; established contributors average 1.45. No new contributor has had a fixed reviewer yet. lumi comes closest: their first two changes both went to aino (2 rounds each), and the third went to dara (3 rounds). That's too little to prove anything.

**Sheet.** It was committed before the change, as the skill requires:
- **decision:** keep the section if the new average is 2.0 rounds or lower; otherwise revert it and record the experiment as failed.
- **control:** the same measurement on those 7 changes from before the rule existed.
- **n:** 7.

I chose that because splitting new contributors into two groups would make the method contradict itself for half of them.
